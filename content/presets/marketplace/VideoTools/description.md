A toolbox for videos you already have. **Interpolate** multiplies a clip's frame rate 2x or 4x: RIFE synthesizes the in-between frames, the duration stays the same, and the audio track is carried over. **Resize** makes a clip smaller and lighter: pick Original, a common height (1080p down to 360p) or a percentage — all worked out from the video's own aspect ratio — or type a custom size, and choose a quality; the frame rate and sound are kept and the video is never stretched or enlarged. For interpolation, the RIFE checkpoint (rife46–rife49 family) lives under `models/vfi/` and is fetched like any other model via the admin downloader. Interpolation is powered by RIFE (Practical-RIFE, hzwer — MIT licensed), vendored under `vendor/rife/`.
