Turn one image into a 3D Gaussian splat scene you can move a camera through. MoGe-2 estimates the depth and the camera of the picture, the scene is cut where depth jumps, the background hidden behind the foreground is filled in, and every pixel becomes a Gaussian. Good for small camera moves around the original viewpoint: captures from a new angle and short orbit, dolly or crane shots. Runs in seconds on a few GB of VRAM.
