Generate a video from a start image, or from a first and a last image, with Wan 2.2's 14B image-to-video experts in 4 steps, set up for a graphics card with **12 GB of VRAM** and **64 GB of system RAM**. It is the image-to-video companion of **Wan 2.2 14B Lightning (12 GB)**: clips of up to 5 seconds at 16 fps, 832×480 by default and up to 960×544, with CFG 1. Count about 2.5 minutes for a 5-second clip. It has no text-to-video mode.

**In Video Producer.** You do not need to pick it. A shot whose engine is Wan 2.2 14B Lightning (12 GB) and that starts from an image is generated with this preset, and its takes name it. A shot without an image goes back to Wan 2.2 14B Lightning (12 GB).

**Files.** The 14B image-to-video high-noise and low-noise experts (fp8-scaled, 13.31 GB each), the fp8 UMT5-XXL text encoder, the Wan 2.1 VAE, and the lightx2v 4-step LoRA v1 pair for image to video (1.14 GB each). The text encoder and the VAE are those of Wan 2.2 14B Lightning (12 GB), and the experts those of the core Wan 2.1 / 2.2 preset: the recipe downloads only what is missing.

**Memory.** It loads experts of the same size as the text-to-video preset, so plan on the same 64 GB of system RAM.
