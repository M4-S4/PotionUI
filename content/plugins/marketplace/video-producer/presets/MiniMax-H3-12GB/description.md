Generate a short video and its soundtrack together from one prompt with MiniMax-H3, set up for a graphics card with **12 GB of VRAM** and **64 GB of system RAM**. Clips of up to 15 seconds at a fixed 24 fps, 832×480 by default. You can anchor the first and last frames with your own images. MiniMax-H3 needs no guidance scale and no negative prompt.

**Modes.** **Video** makes a clip from the prompt and, optionally, a first and a last frame. **References** follows up to 9 images, 3 videos and 3 audio tracks: the faces, sets, motion and sounds you give it. **Continue** carries on the end of a video with new footage; Video Producer uses it to chain a shot to the previous one by continuation. There is no Upscale mode: for it, use the base MiniMax-H3 preset on a larger card.

**Before the first run.** The 32B text encoder is larger than the card. Set the text encoder placement of the native backend to stream it from system RAM (Admin › Backends); otherwise it runs out of GPU memory before sampling starts. Streaming makes each step slower than on a card that holds everything: count several minutes for a 5-second draft on a 12 GB card.

**Files.** The model pickers recommend the int8 ConvRot DiT, the nvfp4 text encoder, the int8 ConvRot video VAE and the fp32 audio VAE. The References mode needs its own DiT, the Ref2VA one (`minimax_h3_ref2va_pruned_int8_convrot.safetensors` from Comfy-Org, or `MiniMax-H3-Ref2VA-pruned_rank8_int8_convrot.safetensors` from Wan2GP); the text encoder and the two VAEs are shared.

**Settings.** The form starts at 832×480 for 124 frames (5.17 s), 20 steps. 480×832, 960×544 and 544×960 are offered, and so is 1344×768, which is far heavier on a 12 GB card. In the References tab, **Reference image short edge** (1088 by default) sets the size each reference picture is encoded at; Video Producer sets it for each take. Leave the two-stage upscale of the Enhance tab off on 12 GB unless you accept a much longer render.
